Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WordWiggle may collect, use and process information when you visit or use the website at wordwiggle.online.

WordWiggle is designed to be simple to use and does not require you to create an account to use its word tools and games.

Information collected automatically

When you visit WordWiggle, certain information may be collected automatically through analytics, cookies and similar technologies.

This information may include details such as your device type, browser type, approximate location, pages visited, interactions with the website, referral source, IP address and other technical information.

This information is generally used to understand how visitors use WordWiggle, improve the website and measure performance.

Google Analytics

WordWiggle uses Google Analytics to help understand how visitors use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information about your visit and interactions with WordWiggle using cookies or similar technologies.

This information may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

WordWiggle and third-party services used by WordWiggle may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, website functionality, advertising and measurement.

You can usually manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features work.

Advertising and Google AdSense

WordWiggle may display advertising through Google AdSense or other advertising services.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to WordWiggle or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on visits to WordWiggle and other websites on the internet.

Advertising providers may also use technologies such as cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to deliver, measure and improve advertisements.

Users can manage personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from websites and apps that use its services by visiting Google's privacy information for partner sites.

Local browser storage

Some WordWiggle features may use your browser's local storage to remember information directly on your device.

For example, Recent Wiggles or similar game preferences may be saved locally so they are available when you return.

This information is stored on your device and is not intended to create a WordWiggle user account.

Challenge links

WordWiggle's Challenge a Friend feature may store puzzle information within the challenge link itself.

Anyone who receives a challenge link may be able to access the puzzle encoded in that link, so users should not enter personal, private or sensitive information when creating challenges.

Children and school use

WordWiggle is designed to be suitable for general audiences, including educational and classroom use.

WordWiggle does not require users to provide a name, email address or account information in order to use its main tools.

Users should not enter personal or sensitive information into WordWiggle's word tools, classroom features or challenge links.

Third-party services

WordWiggle may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms and legal obligations.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as WordWiggle changes, adds new features or uses different services.

When the policy is updated, the revised date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact WordWiggle through the contact information provided on the website.
