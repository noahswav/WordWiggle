What can you do with WordWiggle?

WordWiggle is built for word games, anagrams, classroom activities, puzzles, creative writing and anyone who simply likes playing with letters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WordWiggle?

WordWiggle is a free collection of simple word tools and games for scrambling letters, finding words, making challenges and creating classroom puzzles.

Does Challenge a Friend need an account?

No. The puzzle is safely stored inside the challenge link itself, so no account or database is required.

Can teachers use Classroom Mode?

Yes. Classroom Mode is designed to make simple word puzzles that can be shown on a computer, projector or smartboard.

Is WordWiggle free?

Yes. WordWiggle is free and requires no sign-up.
